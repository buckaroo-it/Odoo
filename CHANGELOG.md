## [19.0.1.0.0]

First official release of the Buckaroo payment provider for Odoo 19.

### Features
- Accept payments through Buckaroo with 30+ payment methods across Europe.
- Express checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal from the product, cart and shop pages, including live shipping selection.
- Refunds from Odoo, full and partial.
- Authorize and capture flow, with capture and cancel from the order.
- Pay Per Email.
- Optional payment surcharges (payment fees).
- Giftcards with partial payment and a paid-amount summary.
- Buy Now, Pay Later: Klarna, in3, Billink and Riverty.
- Credit card payments with support for multiple card brands.
- Apple Pay domain association served on the `.well-known` route.
- Webhook (push) handling to keep transaction and order status in sync.
- Available in English, Dutch, German and French.
