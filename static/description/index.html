Buckaroo Payments for Odoo

A simple, effective solution for handling payments in your Odoo webshop — one Buckaroo account, 30+ SEPA-proof payment methods across Europe.

About

The official Buckaroo payment provider for Odoo 19 supports a wide range of SEPA-compliant and international payment methods, is easy to install, and saves you valuable time. Improve your checkout experience and increase conversions — try the Buckaroo plugin today!

Key features

Express checkout

Let customers pay in one tap from the cart and product pages with Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.

Refunds from Odoo

Issue full or partial refunds directly from the Odoo payment, without logging into Buckaroo Plaza.

Authorize & capture

Authorize now and capture later for Klarna, Riverty and credit cards, so you charge once the order ships.

Payment surcharges

Optionally add a configurable surcharge per payment method to cover transaction costs.

Requirements

  • A Buckaroo account — create one (EN) or aanmaken (NL).
  • Odoo 19 with the Payment and eCommerce apps installed.

Installation & configuration

  1. Install the module from Apps.
  2. Go to Accounting → Configuration → Payment Providers and open Buckaroo Official.
  3. Enter your Website Key and Secret Key from Buckaroo Plaza.
  4. Set the State to Test or Enabled.
  5. Enable the payment methods you want to offer, then save.

Full step-by-step guides are on docs.buckaroo.io.
