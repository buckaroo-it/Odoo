Buckaroo Payments for Odoo

A simple, effective solution for handling payments in your Odoo webshop — One Buckaroo account to offer 30+ local and global payment methods to your customers.

About

The official Buckaroo payment provider for Odoo 19 supports a wide range of SEPA-compliant and international payment methods, is easy to install, and saves you valuable time. Improve your checkout experience and increase conversions — try the Buckaroo plugin today!

Key features

Express checkout

Let customers check out in one tap with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.

Refunds from Odoo

Issue full or partial refunds directly from Odoo, without logging into Buckaroo Plaza.

Authorize & capture

Authorize payments at checkout and charge customers when their order ships with Klarna, Riverty, and credit cards.

Payment surcharges

Optionally add a configurable surcharge per payment method to help cover transaction costs.

Requirements

  • A Buckaroo account — sign up at https://www.buckaroo.eu/sign-up (EN) or https://www.buckaroo.nl/account-aanmaken (NL).
  • Odoo 19 with the Payment and eCommerce apps installed.

Installation & configuration

  1. Install the module from Apps.
  2. Go to Accounting → Configuration → Payment Providers and open Buckaroo Official.
  3. Enter your Store Key and Secret Key from the Buckaroo Plaza.
  4. Set the State to Test or Enabled.
  5. Enable the payment methods you want to offer, then save.

Full step-by-step guides are on https://docs.buckaroo.io/docs/plugins
