## About

The **Buckaroo Payments Plugin for Odoo** connects your Odoo webshop with the Buckaroo payment platform, allowing you to offer customers a fast, secure, and seamless checkout experience.

With one Buckaroo account, merchants can offer **30+ local and international payment methods**, including iDEAL, Bancontact, credit cards, PayPal, Klarna, Riverty, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and more.

The plugin integrates directly with Odoo's payment framework and is designed for a simple setup, reliable payment processing, and efficient order management.

Whether you run Odoo Online, Odoo.sh, or a self-hosted installation, Buckaroo helps you provide customers with the payment options they expect.
